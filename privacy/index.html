The short version

PomoPlan has no advertising SDKs, does not use third-party analytics, and does not require a PomoPlan account. Focus data is stored on your devices and, if iCloud sync is enabled, through Apple in your private iCloud database.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the PomoPlan applications for iPhone and Apple Watch, PomoPlan widgets and complications, and the website at pomoplan.io (together, the “Services”).

“PomoPlan,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the independent publisher of PomoPlan. If you have a privacy question, contact support@pomoplan.io.

PomoPlan is designed to work without creating a PomoPlan account. We do not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or operate an advertising or behavioral-analytics backend.

2. Data PomoPlan handles

The app handles information you enter and information needed to run the focus system. The exact data depends on which features you use.

Plans and contentTopic names, task or note text, task order, multipliers, and whether a task or session is marked casual.
Timer activityFocus and break durations, session timestamps and status, pauses, completion state, and the device needed to keep an active timer synchronized.
Focus insightsSession history, focused time, distracted time, pause counts, focus scores, streaks, and summaries derived from your sessions.
PreferencesTimer defaults, appearance, grace-period and penalty settings, onboarding state, notification preferences, and other app settings.
Focus Guard choicesThe app and category selections you choose through Apple’s Screen Time interface, represented by Apple-provided authorization and selection data.
Support messagesThe email address, message, attachments, and technical details you choose to send when contacting support.

PomoPlan does not request your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone for its core focus features.

3. Where data is stored and synchronized

On your devices

App data is stored locally on your iPhone. A limited current-timer snapshot and settings may be stored in an Apple App Group so the PomoPlan app, widgets, and extensions on that device can show the same state.

Between iPhone and Apple Watch

PomoPlan uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework to send relevant timer state, tasks, settings, and session updates between your paired devices. This communication is provided and secured by Apple’s operating systems.

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is enabled and available, supported PomoPlan data is synchronized using Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services. PomoPlan uses the user’s private CloudKit database. Apple states that, by default, only the user can access this database, the user owns its contents, and its data is not visible in the developer portal. iCloud storage and processing are governed by your Apple Account settings and Apple’s terms.

Learn more in Apple’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s documentation for the private CloudKit database.

4. Apple permissions and system services

PomoPlan asks for access only when a related feature needs it. You can decline or later change permissions in Apple’s Settings app.

  • Notifications: used for focus and break completion alerts, reminders, sound, and Time Sensitive presentation when you enable them. Apple controls final delivery, Focus mode behavior, and whether an alert appears on iPhone or Apple Watch.
  • Family Controls / Screen Time: used only if you enable Focus Guard and choose apps or categories to shield during a focus session. PomoPlan does not send those selections to its own server.
  • iCloud: used to synchronize supported app data through your Apple Account when enabled.
  • WatchConnectivity: used to synchronize supported state with your paired Apple Watch.
  • Live Activities and widgets: used to display current timer information outside the main app.

Apple may process device, permission, notification, iCloud, App Store, and diagnostic information under Apple’s own policies. PomoPlan does not control Apple’s independent processing.

5. Website and support email

Website

The PomoPlan website is a static website. It does not include a PomoPlan analytics script, advertising pixels, a contact form, or marketing cookies. We do not build a visitor profile from your use of the site.

The site is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most internet infrastructure providers, Cloudflare may process request and security metadata—such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, browser or device information, and threat signals—to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare may also set strictly necessary security cookies when a security feature requires them. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the address you use, your message, and any attachments or technical details you include. We use that information to answer your request, investigate an issue, maintain support records, and protect the Services. Your email provider and our email provider also process that communication under their own terms.

6. Retention and deletion

  • App data: remains on your devices and, when enabled, in iCloud until you edit or delete it, clear app data, or Apple removes it under your account or service settings.
  • Cleared-data recovery: when you use PomoPlan’s clear-data control, the app first creates an on-device recovery backup. That backup is automatically eligible for removal after 30 days and is not uploaded to a PomoPlan server.
  • Support email: may be retained as long as reasonably needed to respond, document a recurring issue, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes, then deleted when no longer needed.
  • Hosting logs: are retained by Cloudflare according to its service configuration and privacy policy.

You can delete sessions and queued tasks in the app and use the data controls in Settings. Because iCloud is an Apple service, deletion or synchronization may require a connection and can take time to propagate across devices.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to enable notifications, Time Sensitive alerts, Focus Guard, iCloud, and related Apple system features. You can also edit or delete app content through PomoPlan’s controls.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights to ask about, access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information we control. Because most PomoPlan app data is held locally or in your private Apple account rather than on a PomoPlan server, the fastest way to exercise control is normally through the app and Apple’s device or iCloud settings. You may still contact us for help.

We will not discriminate against you for making a valid privacy request. We may need enough information to understand the request and verify that we are responding to the right person.

8. Children’s privacy

PomoPlan is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by local law to consent to online services. We do not knowingly operate a system for collecting children’s personal information. If you believe a child sent personal information through support email, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

9. Security

PomoPlan reduces exposure by keeping the core experience local and using Apple’s platform services for device communication and private iCloud synchronization. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your devices and Apple Account protected and updated.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PomoPlan’s features, providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be revised. If a change is material, we will provide notice in a reasonable place, such as the app, this website, or the App Store listing.
