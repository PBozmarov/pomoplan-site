The short version

Good questions.

Need help with a device-specific issue? Visit Support.

Does PomoPlan work without an Apple Watch?+

Yes. The complete focus experience lives on iPhone. The Apple Watch app, widgets, and complications make the same timer easier to reach from your wrist.

Is iCloud required?+

No. PomoPlan works with local data on one device. iCloud is used to keep supported data current across your Apple devices when you enable it.

Does it block every app?+

No. App shielding is optional. You choose which apps or categories to shield, and you can change that selection in PomoPlan's settings.

When can I download it?+

PomoPlan is being prepared for release. The App Store link will appear here as soon as the listing is ready.
